## Shared prompt

Polished painterly 3D toy illustration, rich jade, warm ivory and copper, readable chunky silhouettes, soft upper-left highlights, no dark baked background, actual transparent alpha. No text, numerals, UI, watermarks, floor shadow, diagrams, or other objects. One horizontal strip of THREE equal square cells, each object centered at identical scale with 4% transparent padding and almost filling its square tile. Orthographic slightly top-down view, crisp clear silhouette at 40px. Three states from intact to nearly broken. Keep pose and base aligned across the states.
