## Shared direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Original IN-GAME OBSTACLE raster sprites for a premium casual match-three puzzle. Match the EXISTING GAME ART in supplied references: image1 our terracotta pot with raised cream rim and turquoise inset, image2 our urn/statue/crate/crystal sprites. Those references are the ART DIRECTION, especially their gently hand-painted ceramic surfaces, substantial sculpted thickness, warm bounce lighting, slightly top-down frontal camera and readable broad highlights. Create a believable tactile game prop that sits on a puzzle board. Moderately stylized painted 2.5D: detailed enough to feel like a real ceramic/metal/stone object, with subtle irregular brushed shading, broad bevels, small rim wear and inner occlusion. Keep only one or two BIG decorative shapes, no miniature patterns or tiny flowers. NOT a flat vector symbol, NOT an emoji, NOT an app icon, NOT perfectly smooth plastic toy shading, NOT photoreal jewelry or an ornate miniature diorama. Upper-left warm light, shaded lower-right, clear visible top rim and body depth. Bold broad silhouette fills a tile with very little empty space and reads at 38px. TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA exterior, no floor, background, outside drop shadow, captions, letters, digits, watermarks, UI, glitter, floating sparkles or aura. Materials and palette must fit our existing urn: terracotta, warm cream, jade and softly painted highlights.
