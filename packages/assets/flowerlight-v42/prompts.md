# Flowerlight v42 generated assets

All four assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Runtime images were resized/encoded with Sharp; the fountain retains real alpha. Source files were kept. No existing asset was overwritten.

## festival-plaza.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished landscape background illustration for an original mobile match-three game's Flowerlight Festival restoration scene, aspect ratio 3:2. A small floating garden clearing at dusk with a distant glass greenhouse, warm peach sky, deep cornflower-blue shadows, soft olive grass, hydrangea and hibiscus edges. View from gently elevated 3/4 angle. The central 65 percent must be a simple EMPTY stone round plaza with room for separately composited fountain, flower pots, arch and visitors. No central fountain, no characters, no built decorations on the clearing. Warm, inviting, rich colors, glossy sculpted casual game illustration, crisp silhouettes, restrained bloom, no blinding highlights. Full bleed background; no text, no frames, no interface, no lettering. Original environment, not a copy of an existing game.

## flowerlight-fountain.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Single original game decoration sprite for the Flowerlight Festival reward in a casual mobile garden game. A small magical FLOWER LANTERN FOUNTAIN, full object centered on true transparent background. Isometric 3/4 front view, elevation 30 degrees, square diamond footprint. Three scalloped warm sandstone basins shaped like five-petal blossoms, vivid deep blue water, elegant copper-gold trim, blush and coral flowers on rim, five small amber-glowing glass flower lanterns surrounding the basin. A large luminous warm amber flower atop the fountain. Chunky sculpted 3D hand-painted casual-game finish, soft material highlights, strong readable silhouette at 120px, restrained glow inside the silhouette, no white bloom outside. Entire base visible, generous transparent margins. No text, no UI, no ground/background, no cast shadow beyond base. Original design.

## flowerlight-fountain-rear.png

Reference/edit target: generated front fountain.

Use case: precise-object-edit. This is the FRONT view of one game decoration. Make its REAR view, rotating the entire identical object 180 degrees around its vertical axis while keeping the same isometric camera/elevation and same scale/base diamond framing. Preserve the three flower basins, warm sandstone, copper-gold trim, five flower lanterns, blue water, coral flowers, and large amber flower. The large top flower shows its copper and green structural BACK, not the front glowing petals. Waterfalls and lanterns seen naturally from the opposite side. Single isolated centered whole sprite on true transparent background. No text, no UI, no ground, no extra objects. Do not merely mirror the front image.
